# W7 delivery summary

All **24 assigned IDs** were rewritten, built offline with `tools/models/build.sh`, and visually reviewed against their design/compare images. Eight regular build/review passes were run per ID; the final attachment audit added one repair rebuild each for flower-heart, fern, page-bird and mushroom. Final scripts read their scalar largest-extent target from the passed kit spec. No shared pipeline/design/catalog files or other workers’ asset scripts were edited.

All final models meet the kit triangle-use range (50–100%), remain within 5% of the scalar size target, use only shared-palette hexes, and export with vertex colors and exactly `pivot_root`. Validation: [w7-validation.json](w7-validation.json). Source-palette checks precede the pipeline's allowed lighting bake.

**Silhouette acceptance remains incomplete.** The per-item numbers below are the unmodified pipeline results, including failed scores. Only star-decal meets both available front/top IoU thresholds; its continuous gold rim is still simplified. Side IoU is **N/A for every item because the kit directories contain no side image**. No item is being represented as having passed all three measured views. The iteration cap was reached; missing side references, neighboring-item contamination, clipped kit cells, pale-color mask holes, inconsistent top poses, and genuine remaining budget-limited shape differences are detailed in [w7-reference-notes.md](w7-reference-notes.md).

`size Δ` below means `max(actual X,Y,Z) / spec.items[i].size_m - 1`; kit specs do not provide three separate target axes. Every line includes the final source's deliberate simplifications. Final iso overview: [w7-model-overview.jpg](w7-model-overview.jpg). Detailed per-ID sheets are in `deliverables/omg-c-models/<id>-compare.jpg`.
