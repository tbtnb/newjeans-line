Review limitations: H11 design foreground extraction leaks through some cream base/column/ear areas. H12 top is explicitly angled in spec and is not an acceptance target. H15 spec records 11.9% height inconsistency between reference views; the side and top aspect ratios also disagree with the exact size_m dimensions. H16 foreground extraction fills enclosed column openings in the reference but the model retains genuine transparent gaps; its side stairs are drawn on the opposite side to the prescribed +X camera. These observations do not turn the failed raw metrics into passes. `build/W3-filled-silhouette-advisory.json` is diagnostic only; it does not replace the pipeline metrics.
