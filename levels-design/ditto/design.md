## Concept / grounding
- Song: warm vocals over a light, urgent Baltimore/Jersey-club drum kit; lyric = waiting for the other person to answer your feelings. Stem maps: choir intro; drums drop out completely twice (55.8–64.8 s, 114.9–129.3 s) — those become the "stillness" spaces.
- MV (Side A/B): school, hand-held camcorder, watching tapes back, plaster-cast arm, winter deer. Director's interview: relationships change and end; the deer in the snow was a starting image. Level = walking through a tape of the school in winter: crowded memories open into white silence and are kept, not owned.
- Spatial idea: long straights (waiting) → pause-like corners → open "answer" spaces; campus blocks floating in fog at three heights (H0 campus, H1 = +3 roof, H2 ≈ +6 bridge) plus the snow level at −4.

## Landmarks & eggs
Landmarks: cassette start & end boxes, silver camcorder with flip screen, classroom blocks + lockers, rooftop empty chairs, deer snow island + turning deer, bridge B1, halftone bunny relief (cover), warm windows.
Eggs: **E1** (≈130–135 s) an off-road camcorder screen shows a *sixth* chair that is not on the real rooftop; **E2** a white wristband on the camcorder strap (cast-arm nod) near 34 s. Both off-road, no branch, no input change.

## Collectibles
Diamonds: 8.8, 23.9, 45.5, 58.2, 81.3, 110.0, 120.4, 136.6, 160.9, 175.4 s (straights ≥ 0.25 s from taps). Crowns: 59.8 s (snow break) and 126.6 s (end of bridge), finish = 3rd.

## Readability
No hints (no markers, pulses, countdowns, guide lines). Locker rows react to the player's real taps only; everything else reacts to beat/kick. Reveal lead ≥ 1.4 s (fade 2.0 s in the drumless parts, rise 1.4 s in the finale). Taps in drumless sections are ≥ 1 beat (448 ms) apart.
