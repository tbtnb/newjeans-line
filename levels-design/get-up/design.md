# Get Up: After the Rain (影子庭院)

**Concept.** Get Up is a 36 s interlude about getting back on your feet after a loss: "get up, walk on". It is the bridge between Cool With You's rain and ASAP's meadow. The line walks out of shallow rain water, through a shadow alley toward one warm light, climbs while the harmonies gather, and drops onto moon-green grass under five coloured light beams.

The Codex reference ("Shadow Courtyard": shadow alley, long ramp, five folded white wings, five-colour finale, lavender/teal palette) sets the mood. The route is ours: four short, distinct textures with one over/under knot, at an easy-tier difficulty. The whole 36.26 s file is used.

- Palette: teal sky #A8C8D1, lavender #B8B4D5, shadow #544D70, road #F5F1E8, pink line #EC679D.
- Cover colours: #4B9EDF #93CF9B #F1A9C9 #E9BC83 #B39AD7.
- Speed: 7.5 u/s.
- Road width: 2.8 → **2.0** (alley) → 2.4 → 2.6 → 2.8.
- Elevation: 0 to +3.5. 9 legal crossings.
