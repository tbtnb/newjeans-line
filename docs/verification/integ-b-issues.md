# integ-b: issues and blockers (integrator B: super-shy, eta, cool-with-you, get-up, asap)

No engine code (`src/`) was modified. New files are per-song scripts (`tools/charts/{super_shy,eta,cool_with_you,get_up,asap}.py`) plus integ-b helpers (`tools/charts/integb_*.py`) that no other pipeline imports. No registry change was needed (`src/catalog.ts` globs `src/levels/*.json`).

1. **`analysis/asap.grid.json` has the wrong tempo (129.52 BPM).** The full-mix onsets fit 130.0 BPM (97 % of strong onsets within 25 ms of the 16th grid vs 53 % for 129.52), and the grid drifts by about 0.4 s by the end. chartlogic.py then refit a 129.65 BPM grid and reported 95 false off-grid taps. Workaround (per-song only): the ASAP chart declares `meta.bpm 130.012 / beat0 0.341`, and `levels-src/asap/layout.json` carries its own `beats` list, so beat-reactive scenery does not drift. The shared analysis file was not edited; whoever owns `analysis/` should regenerate it.
2. **A removed tap is a removed turn.** Everything after the first removal is rotated by 90°, so the worker's global map cannot be kept verbatim. `integb_route.py` keeps each phrase's own turn sequence where that stays legal: eta 55/58 phrases kept, cool-with-you 33/35, get-up 7/8, asap 20 kept and 2 mirrored out of 26, super-shy 43 kept and 1 mirrored out of 49. worldPos landmarks are moved rigidly with the road frame of the section that circles them. Where the new road ran into a landmark, it was moved to the nearest clear spot: super-shy courtyard_green 6 u, relief_wall 6 u, courtyard_walls_15 1.5 u, courtyard_walls_16 3 u; get-up light_beam_1 1.5 u; asap white_wing_0 3 u. Before/after plans: `deliverables/<slug>/route-before-after.png`.
3. **Super Shy:** W2 charted against a demucs drum stem ("kick drum" in every section). The full-mix band envelopes used by chartlogic (and by the Ditto trim) flagged 14 taps (4 off-layer, 6 faint, 4 weak bursts), so they were trimmed the same way Ditto was: 378 → 364. Side effect: peak 300 → 240 CPM, and 16th bursts 5 → 1. If the user prefers W2's stem-based judgement, `levels-design/super-shy/pre-audit/` is the untouched version.
4. **ASAP Pre / Chorus 1 / Final hook:** most of the worker's taps there sit on onsets that are faint in the full mix (all bands < 0.35; the chorus is soft and pad-led). The audit removed 7+1, 12 and 5+2 of them, which leaves Chorus 1 at 0.95 taps/s. This is a mechanical result. Nobody has listened to it, and a human listening pass could re-chart these sections on the vocal hook.
5. **`npm ci` fails** on `origin/rebuild`: package.json and package-lock.json are out of sync. I used `/workspace/nj-integ-a/node_modules` via a symlink, excluded from git.
6. **vocal envelopes:** `tools/audio/vocal_env.py` was run for the five songs. It adds a `vocal` key to `analysis/<slug>.env.npz`, which is committed. chartlogic then uses it for `follows: vocal` sections.
7. Playthroughs are **scripted simulations** (CDP touches at chart times, headless, software rendering at ~10–20 fps). They prove the chart and the route are completable, not that it feels right on a phone or that A/V is in sync.
8. **Event `target` arrays crash level load.** W3's cool-with-you and get-up layouts use list targets (`"target": ["observer_0", …]`). The engine's `targets()` expects a string, so loading threw `e.endsWith is not a function`, showed a toast, and `audio.ready` never became true; the first playthrough attempt timed out. `validate.py` does not catch this. I fixed the data, not the engine: a list becomes `'prefix*'` when that prefix matches exactly those ids, otherwise one event per id (cool-with-you `city_*` light chain: 8 events offset by `every`). Suggested follow-up for the engine owner: accept arrays in `targets()`, or make the validator reject them.
9. **`play.mjs --shots` can cost a tap.** A screenshot stalls the software-rendered page. The Super Shy run with a shot at 131.0 s failed "late" on the tap at 131.156 s. The same chart had completed 364/364 without shots. Shots are now placed just after a tap, inside the widest nearby gap (`/workspace/nj-integ-b-work/shottimes.py`).
10. **Dressing (v2.6):** each level's roadside props follow its palette and setting (`tools/charts/integb_dressing.py`). Breaks and hushes thin out, and choruses thicken. Props pop in 2.6 s ahead of the line, so a frame can show the road ahead still bare. The density has only been judged from screenshots, not on a phone.
11. **ASAP audit refinement:** four flagged taps were one 16th away from a clear hit of their own section's layer, so they were re-timed instead of dropped: 158 → 162. The tap-by-tap reasons are in `docs/verification/asap-audit-rationale.md`.
